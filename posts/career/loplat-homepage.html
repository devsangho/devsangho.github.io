The homepage introduces Loplat's main products, press coverage, job postings, and blog content.

I maintained the existing NuxtJS and Sass codebase for one year. Then, following a homepage redesign plan, I rebuilt it using NextJS and loplat UI.

Implemented mobile responsiveness and developed features that allow non-developer staff to manage news articles, blog posts, and job postings that were previously hardcoded.
