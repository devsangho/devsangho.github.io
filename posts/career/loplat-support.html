An internal management system for job postings and news articles.

Period

Oct – Nov 2020

Tech Stack

React, styled-component, ESLint, Firebase

Work

I noticed that job postings and news articles displayed on the homepage were manually hardcoded, so I proposed building a management system. Developed features for logging in with Google accounts, and creating, editing, publishing, and deleting job postings and news articles. Also removed the existing hardcoded content from the homepage and replaced it with dynamically loaded data.
