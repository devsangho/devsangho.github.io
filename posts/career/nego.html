Nego is a service where users search for products and add them to a wishlist. Even if different product listings are selected, the algorithm extracts product IDs and consolidates identical products for unified management. A crawling engine then runs on a set schedule, stores pricing data, and displays price trend graphs. Users receive notifications when their desired price or price fluctuation is detected.

Period

Dec 2015 – Feb 2016

Tech Stack

Swift2, Realm

Work

I maintained the existing iOS app written in Swift2, and developed the price change graph, do-not-disturb mode, and wishlist features using Realm and Storyboard.
